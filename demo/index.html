DigitNet

A neural network written from scratch in NumPy (no PyTorch or TensorFlow), trained on handwritten digits. The trained weights are loaded here and the forward pass runs in your browser.

What the network sees

Your drawing is cropped, centered and shrunk to an 8×8 grid, the same format as the training data. That's only 64 numbers going in. They pass through two hidden layers (128 and 64 ReLU units) and a softmax that turns 10 scores into probabilities.
